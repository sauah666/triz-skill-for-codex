# Code of Conduct

This repository is meant to be useful, practical, and low-drama.

## Expectations

- Be respectful.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Keep feedback specific and useful.
- Avoid harassment, threats, or demeaning behavior.
- Be honest when a technique is weak, expensive, or unnecessary.

## What good collaboration looks like

- direct feedback
- clear reasoning
- willingness to revise when the evidence is better
- no fake politeness, no needless hostility

## Enforcement

If someone behaves in a way that makes collaboration worse or unsafe, the maintainers may close issues, reject PRs, or block participation.

Keep it clean. Keep it useful. Keep it moving.
